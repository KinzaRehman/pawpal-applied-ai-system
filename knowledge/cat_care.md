# Cat Care Guidelines

## Feeding
- Cats should have consistent feeding times.
- Fresh water should always be available.

## Medication
- Medication instructions from a veterinarian always take priority.
- Medication tasks should be completed before lower-priority activities.

## Cleaning
- Litter boxes should be cleaned daily.

## Play and Enrichment
- Cats benefit from daily play and mental stimulation.

## Safety
- PawPal is a scheduling tool, not a substitute for veterinary advice.
- Owners must verify medication timing and dosage.
